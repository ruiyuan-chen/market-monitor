About the data

Update schedule

An automated job runs every weekday at about 5:30 pm Eastern time, after the U.S. market close. Stock prices are end-of-day. The economic, valuation and factor data change only when their sources publish: monthly, with a lag.

    Sources

    • Index, sector, company, commodity, currency and Bitcoin prices: Yahoo Finance, via the open-source yfinance library (unofficial; for research and educational use).
    • Interest rates, inflation, jobs, GDP and recession dates: FRED®, Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis. High-yield spread: ICE Data Indices, LLC, ICE BofA US High Yield Index Option-Adjusted Spread, retrieved from FRED.
    • CAPE and P/E: Robert J. Shiller, online data.
    • Factor returns: Kenneth R. French Data Library.
    • Drawdown risk model: daily prices for about 490 large U.S.-listed stocks and the S&P 500 from Yahoo Finance; model and evaluation by the site's author. Its probabilities are statistical estimates, not recommendations to buy or sell.

    Notes

    This site is for teaching and research. It is not investment advice, and the data may contain errors or delays. Sector and company returns use prices adjusted for dividends where noted. Company metrics (P/E, dividend yield, market cap) are as reported by Yahoo Finance.
